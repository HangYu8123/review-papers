Paper state machine

        
Guideline. Map the argument of the paper, not its pipeline. Start from the key intuition and the key contributions, judged fairly. Then show each experiment with its environment and total workload, linked to the work it tests, with the result on the link and weak links in red. Add other components only when they help to understand the paper or reveal an important weakness.
