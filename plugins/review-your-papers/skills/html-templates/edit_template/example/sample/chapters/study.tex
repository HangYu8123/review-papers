\chapter{A Toy Study: Feedback in a Sorting Task}
\label{ch:study}

\section{Introduction}

We conducted a study with 24 participants to see how people rate a robot
that sorts blocks \cite{toy2024}. Our intuition is that ratings drift as a session goes on.
% We conducted a study with 24 participants (an older, commented-out draft of the line above).

It is worth noting that every participant saw the same twelve clips. The clips were
shown in a random order.

\section{Results}

\subsection{Rating drift}

We found that ratings fell by $0.4 \pm 0.1$ points between the first and the last
block, as shown in \autoref{fig:drift}.

The effect was the same for both groups.

\section{Discussion}

The drift is small. It is, however, consistent across participants.
